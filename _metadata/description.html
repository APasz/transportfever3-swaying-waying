Swaying Waying

Adjustable suspension motion and optional onboard cameras for compatible trains, wagons, coaches, and trams. Vehicle performance and the transport simulation are unchanged.

Features

  • Body roll, pitch, vertical suspension movement, and outward lean in curves.
  • Controls for motion intensity, individual axes, curve lean, track influence, sampling intervals, and vehicle variation.
  • Stable, body-following Sway, or Both onboard views; existing cameras are preserved.
  • Vanilla and modded rail vehicles, preserving compatible animations and scripts.

Onboard cameras

Cameras are disabled by default. Regular modes use a crew viewpoint or an existing camera. + Extra also considers passenger seats or, if no suitable seat or camera exists, an exterior view above the vehicle and its load near the rear end, looking along the vehicle.

One representative viewpoint is selected per vehicle; Both provides Stable and Sway variants of that point. Existing views remain available. With swaying disabled, Stable views still work; Sway views are not added. No other camera mod is required.

Usage and compatibility

Configure the mod when starting a game or loading a save; reload the save to apply changes. Axis balances and curve lean can be disabled separately.

The Sway mode dropdown offers Off, On (default), and Speedless Sway. On varies sway strength with speed; Speedless Sway uses full strength from 5 km/h. All sway and curve lean settle below 5 km/h in both modes. Curve lean remains speed-dependent.

Unsafe model changes are skipped and logged. Compatibility depends on other vehicle mods preserving existing callbacks and camera entries.

Disable Enable Railcar Swaying in James' Reusable US Assets to avoid stacked motion. Compatibility adapters cover Real Track Cant, Realistic Environment Pro (formerly Just More Weather), and Auto Passenger Cameras (formerly Wagon Cameras); these mods are optional.

Cayde's Track Cant is incompatible with Swaying Waying. Disable one of the two mods. The original Real Track Cant remains supported.
